How Indian families use this page

Treat this guide as a decision worksheet — not a brochure. Compare the fee bands and eligibility notes with two or three peer destinations, list documents you already have (marksheets, NEET/English scores, passport), and only then shortlist universities or consultants. LAUNCHPAD pairs this research layer with WhatsApp counselling and AI agents so you move from tabs of articles to an actual application calendar.

Checklist before you pay any booking amount

  1. Separate tuition, hostel, mess, insurance, visa, and agent fees in writing.
  2. Confirm medium of instruction, intake month, and refund policy.
  3. Match eligibility to current Indian regulatory notes (especially for medicine).
  4. Align education-loan or family-fund proof with visa timelines.
  5. Keep a parallel Plan-B destination or India pathway if seats slip.
