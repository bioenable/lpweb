Browse the complete LAUNCHPAD directory

This index brings the public pages together by the decision they support. Start with the admissions marketplace if you are deciding what kind of help to explore; use courses and programs to begin with a subject, or destinations to compare places. The directory below links to every page listed in the XML sitemap, including the home page, so a reader can move from a broad question to a relevant detail page without relying on a search result.

Use the program and destination groups to create a small shortlist, then compare the same factors for each choice: entry requirements, intake timing, tuition period, living expenses and documents that still need confirmation. The medicine group collects MBBS, NEET and related material; exam pages are separated so test planning does not get mixed up with course requirements. Funding, visa and arrival pages cover practical steps that often depend on the chosen institution and destination.

When a research page leaves a personal question unresolved, the services index shows the types of support described on this site and counselling provides a route to discuss next steps. Check current institutional and authority instructions before making a payment or relying on a general guide. Pages are grouped for navigation, not ranked by suitability, and a link is not a guarantee of admission, funding or a visa.

To keep the research actionable, make a note for each page you open: when it was checked, what still needs official confirmation, and the next step that belongs to the student or family. The directory does not imply that every listed route fits every applicant. It is broad so a reader can find relevant material from a shared index, while the final shortlist should reflect the actual course, academic record, budget and timeline. Revisit time-sensitive requirements directly with the institution or authority before making a decision.
